# Opposite-first selected-chain classification

**Historical intermediate classification.** The later half-shift trace
obstruction in `global_lower_bound6_complete_filter.md` excludes the
opposite-pair images left by this calculation and completes the global
six-CNOT lower bound.

This bounded calculation concerns the 88 five-CNOT pair schedules left by
`global_lower_bound6_adjacent_v_certificate.md`. It does not prove a
six-CNOT lower bound; the global interval remains
(5\leq C_{\min}\leq14).

Consider a degree-two wire (j) whose second incident CNOT is fourth and
joins the physically opposite wire (k). The fifth CNOT joins (k) to
(\ell), which is adjacent to (j). The selected input Pauli axis that
commutes through its first CNOT has a final image in the same
21-dimensional overapproximate space as the preceding chain cases. A
collective rotation fixes the final wire-(\ell) axis to (Z_\ell).
Take the representative physical chain ((j,k,\ell)=(0,2,1)):

\[
\mathcal L_{021}=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}
\{\sigma_\mu^{(0)},\;\sigma_\mu^{(0)}\sigma_\nu^{(2)},\;
\sigma_\mu^{(0)}\sigma_\nu^{(2)}Z_1:
\mu,\nu\in\{X,Y,Z\}\}.
\]

The exact spectral-path Gram matrix for this basis, generated by
`global_lower_bound6_opposite_chain_gram.py`, has rational rank 189
among 231 quadratic monomials (ledger run 212). It has genuine nonzero
spectral-path solutions. `global_lower_bound6_opposite_chain_certificate.py`
checks the rank-one equations exactly over (\mathbb Q), after adding
(\sum x_i^2=1), which every nonzero real solution may satisfy by
rescaling (ledger run 215). The exact nullspace first removes all three
one-body coefficients and five of the nine three-body coefficients. The
Gröbner basis then shows that the squares of the remaining four
three-body coefficients vanish. It also forces symmetry of the
(3\times3) coefficient matrix of the opposite-pair terms:

\[
x_{X_0Y_2}=x_{Y_0X_2},\quad
x_{X_0Z_2}=x_{Z_0X_2},\quad
x_{Y_0Z_2}=x_{Z_0Y_2}.
\]

Consequently every **real** spectral-path solution in
(\mathcal L_{021}) has support only on the opposite pair (0,2) and
commutes with (V_4^2), which swaps those wires. This is a necessary
condition on the selected physical image, independent of CNOT directions
and local gates. The selected input Pauli axis is off-diagonal: if it
were (\pm Z_j), its proper-support physical image would commute with
(V_4), contradicting the full-support lemma. Thus the diagonal (D^2)
is constant under flipping input bit (j).

This condition does **not** exclude these schedules. The exact image
(X_0X_2\in\mathcal L_{021}) satisfies the spectral-path equations and
commutes with (V_4^2); it is an image that a suitable pair of CNOT
orientations can preserve. Of the 88 currently unexcluded schedules,
48 have this opposite-first penultimate pattern: 24 five-simple-edge
(3322) schedules and 24 one-doubled-edge (3322) schedules. None has
a second terminal opposite selected axis, so the known (10,6)
multiplicity contradiction does not follow. The remaining 40 have a
degree-two wire whose second incident CNOT occurs at position three.
Whether the other degree-two selected axis in the 48 must also commute
with (D^2) remains open; its image can spread through two later CNOTs.
