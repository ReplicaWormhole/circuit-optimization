# Adjacent V-shaped selected-axis obstruction

**Historical intermediate certificate.** The complete schedule proof in
`global_lower_bound6_complete_filter.md` establishes the current global
interval \(6\leq C_{\min}\leq14\). This certificate remains one part of it.

The global bound for ancilla-free diagonalization of the four-qubit shift
remains (5\leq C_{\min}\leq14). This exact finite certificate extends
`global_lower_bound6_chain_certificate.md` to a second physical chain
shape, leaving arbitrary one-qubit gates, CNOT directions and pairs, output
eigenvalue order, and degenerate-sector basis choices free.

Suppose a degree-two wire (j) has its second CNOT in chronological
position four on pair (j,k), and the fifth CNOT joins (k,\ell). Both
(j,k) and (j,\ell) are adjacent under the physical four-cycle; (k)
and (\ell) are opposite. The input Pauli axis that commutes through the
first incident CNOT on (j) has a final physical image in the same
21-dimensional overapproximate space described in the previous note,
now represented by ((j,k,\ell)=(0,1,3)):

\[
\mathcal L_{013}=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}
\{\sigma_\mu^{(0)},\;\sigma_\mu^{(0)}\sigma_\nu^{(1)},\;
\sigma_\mu^{(0)}\sigma_\nu^{(1)}Z_3:
\mu,\nu\in\{X,Y,Z\}\}.
\]

A collective rotation commuting with (V_4) fixes the last wire's
single Pauli axis to (Z_3). The overapproximation includes every local
gate choice and either CNOT orientation.

With (M_a=4P_a), compute the spectral-path quartic

\[
F(A)=\sum_{a,b,c\;\mathrm{distinct}}\lVert M_aAM_bAM_c\rVert_F^2.
\]

For 21 real coefficients (x_i), let (m(x)) be the 231 quadratic
monomials (x_ix_j), (i\leq j). The exact integer Gram matrix (G)
with (F(A)=m(x)^\mathsf TGm(x)) is generated by
`global_lower_bound6_adjacent_v_gram.py` and saved in its result JSON
(ledger run 207). Its exact rational rank is 210, with a 21-dimensional
formal kernel. Because (G) is a Gram matrix, any (F(A)=0) must satisfy
(Gm(x)=0). Exact nullspace analysis forces eight coefficient squares to
vanish. On the remaining 13 coefficients, the exact Gröbner basis of
(Gm(x)=0) together with (\sum x_i^2=1) is \(\{1\}\). Thus no nonzero
real (A\in\mathcal L_{013}) satisfies the spectral-path identity.
`global_lower_bound6_adjacent_v_certificate.py` recomputes that
certificate over SymPy rationals and enumerates the schedules (ledger
run 208). As in the prior chain calculation, the Gaussian-integer Gram
entries are exactly represented by complex128 arithmetic: each
quadratic image entry has magnitude at most 128 and each Gram entry is
below (24\cdot256\cdot128^2<2^{27}).

adjacent to (0,1,3); reflection only permutes the spectral labels
